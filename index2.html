What is Inner Path?

A space of return

Inner Path is an invitation to return to yourself — to your body, your feelings, and your deepest truth. Through different practices, an opening is created where what is internal can show itself with clarity, without judgment and without rush.

It's a place where the body speaks, emotion moves, and something deeper begins to guide.

The body speaks — and here it is heard.

Something deeper than the mind begins to guide.
No rush. No judgment. No need to become someone else.

The Heart of the Work

Soul Therapy

An approach that integrates different tools to access the deep information of each person — emotional, energetic and of the soul. Not about analyzing or correcting — but opening a space where what lives inside can express itself.

🔮

What is Soul?

In this work, Soul is not abstract. It is the deep intelligence that inhabits each person — the part that knows, feels, and remembers.

🌊

It guides without imposing

It shows without forcing. It reveals what is ready to be seen. The soul is not analyzed — the soul is listened to.

✨

Each process is unique

Respecting the rhythm, history, and truth of each person. Every process unfolds differently, organically, at its own pace.

Practices

The therapies

Within Soul Therapy, the work unfolds through different therapies that allow entry to the experience from different levels. Each one opens a unique door to the internal world.

01 Soul Channeling
Deep Opening ▼

A space of deep opening guided by the therapist. The therapist places their hands over the heart of the patient, generating a direct point of contact that sustains, contains, and opens the internal space. An eyemask accompanies this process, helping the experience become even more internal, perceptive and connected. From this place, images, sensations and messages begin to manifest from a deeper level of consciousness.

  • Presence of transcended souls or souls still in human experience can be invited
  • Can take the form of a family constellation — showing what was hidden
  • Access to memories, experiences or past lives
  • The invisible becomes visible; the soul finds its way to communicate
  • The therapist guides and holds the space with presence and sensitivity
02 Soul Mapping
Language as Door ▼

A therapy that explores the internal world through language. The person arrives with a theme, a concern or an emotion, and from there a guided process opens where each word begins to reveal information. A living map is created of what the person says, feels and repeats — a fabric of language showing how their inner experience is structured.

  • Words are not casual — they emerge from the conscious, subconscious and unconscious
  • Patterns identified, blockages revealed, emotional and family dynamics made visible
  • Family system bonds, loyalties and inherited structures can emerge
  • Interpretation comes from deep listening, not the analytical mind
  • What was confused becomes ordered; what was hidden begins to show itself
03 Soul Tantra Activation
Energy & Expansion ▼

A space of energetic activation and expansion of consciousness. This work focuses on the vital energy of the body known as kundalini — an energy at the base of the spine that, when activated, begins to ascend naturally through the body, traveling through the energetic centers (chakras), which are points where energy organizes, blocks or expresses itself.

  • Greater sensitivity in the body and emotional opening
  • Liberation of blockages and expansion of consciousness
  • A deeper connection with yourself
  • Not forced — through breath, presence and body work, energy awakens organically
  • Creates space for energy to express, reorganize and find its own path
04 Soul Breathwork
Holotropic · 3 Modalities ▼

Based on holotropic breathing — a circular, continuous and conscious breath that allows access to expanded states of perception. Through this breathing, vital energy activates, stored emotions release, the body unblocks, deep memories surface, and the nervous system enters a process of reorganization. Safe and accompanied at all times.

🌍 Earth Breathwork

Lying down on a mat, body fully supported in a safe space. Holotropic breathing opens and releases what has been contained. Specific music accompanies the depth of the experience.

🪞 Mirror Breathwork

Begins facing a mirror, holding your own gaze. Emotions, judgments, patterns and layers of identity emerge. Then continues lying down with eyemask in holotropic breath, deepening and integrating what opened.

💧 Water Breathwork

Performed in a pool. The person, with eyemask, is held at all times by the therapist. The body floats while holotropic breathing guides a deeply immersive experience of surrender and release.

05 Soul Guidance
Clarity & Integration ▼

A space of clarity, understanding and integration. Soul Guidance is an accompaniment where the lived experience begins to take form through words — ordering, comprehending, and giving meaning to what has emerged. A conscious dialogue between therapist and patient, integrating the emotional with mental clarity.

  • Cognitive-behavioral and rational-emotive perspectives
  • Semiological and family constellation approaches
  • What is lived can be recognized, named and understood
  • Experience is not only felt — it is integrated
  • The bridge between the inner experience and the consciousness that sustains it
